Privacy Policy

How AntiScam Wizard handles the information you choose to submit.

Effective date: September 25, 2026

AntiScam Wizard is designed to help you evaluate suspicious messages, emails, links, screenshots, and QR codes. This policy explains the information involved when you use the AntiScam Wizard website.

Information you submit

AntiScam Wizard processes information you choose to submit so it can provide scam risk analysis and safety guidance. Depending on the feature you use, this may include text, links, screenshots, images, or QR-code content.

Do not submit highly sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, one-time security codes, financial account numbers, payment-card numbers, recovery phrases, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information. When possible, remove unnecessary personal information before submitting content for analysis.

How submitted information is used

Information you submit is used to perform the requested analysis, identify potential scam or phishing warning signs, explain the result, and provide practical safety guidance.

Automated results

No automated system can guarantee that a message, website, sender, or request is legitimate or fraudulent. AntiScam Wizard results are informational and should be combined with independent verification. When money, accounts, identity, or personal safety may be involved, contact the organization through its official website, app, or a phone number you already trust.

Third-party services and links

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies. AntiScam Wizard is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites you choose to visit.

Children's privacy

AntiScam Wizard is not intended to knowingly collect highly sensitive personal information from children. Users should avoid submitting personal or sensitive information that is not necessary for scam analysis.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as AntiScam Wizard changes or adds features. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised.

Contact

For privacy questions about AntiScam Wizard, use the contact information provided on the AntiScam Wizard website.

Frequently asked questions

How AntiScam Wizard helps you check for scams

Quick answers about checking suspicious texts, phishing links, screenshots, and QR codes.

What can AntiScam Wizard check?

AntiScam Wizard can analyze suspicious text messages, emails, website links, screenshots with readable text, marketplace offers, payment requests, job messages, and QR-code destinations for common scam and phishing warning signs.

Can it tell me whether a message is definitely safe?

No automated scam detector can guarantee that a message is legitimate or fraudulent. AntiScam Wizard explains the evidence it finds, shows uncertainty when evidence is limited, and recommends independent verification through an official website, app, or trusted phone number.

Should I open a suspicious link before checking it?

No. Avoid opening an unfamiliar link just to investigate it. Copy the link without visiting it when possible, then verify the sender and destination independently.

How does the QR code checker help?

The QR code checker lets you inspect a code’s destination and surrounding context before choosing whether to open the link. A QR code can hide a misleading or unexpected website address.

What should I do if a message looks like a scam?

Do not click its links, open attachments, send money, or provide passwords or security codes. Contact the organization through its official app, website, or a phone number you already trust, then block or report the sender when appropriate.

Privacy and responsible use

AntiScam Wizard processes information you choose to submit so it can provide risk analysis and safety guidance. Do not submit passwords, one-time security codes, financial account numbers, recovery phrases, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information.

No automated system can guarantee that a message is legitimate or fraudulent. Results are informational and should be combined with independent verification. When money, accounts, identity, or personal safety may be involved, contact the organization using an official website, app, or trusted phone number.

How to check a suspicious message

  1. Paste the text or link, or upload an image.
  2. Select “Check for scam” to review the message.
  3. Read the reasons and verify important requests through the organization’s official website, app or a phone number you already trust.

Never enter a password, payment details or a security code just because a message asks you to.
